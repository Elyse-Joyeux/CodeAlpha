Product image notes
===================

The storefront maps the sample image filenames from seed.js to Unsplash image
URLs in ../js/api.js. Existing database records will therefore show the new
photography without rerunning the seed script.

For custom products, the image field may be either a filename saved in this
folder or a full image URL. If a local image is used, save a square JPG or PNG
here and store its filename on the product. Missing images use the built-in
placeholder.
